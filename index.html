Settings and mount setup

Calibrate jig with a reference shim

Caliper one shim's outer length and width. Measure it on the jig, tap Add calibration photo, then move the shim slightly and repeat 2–3 times. The app sets the dot spacing so that shim reads its true size. No need to measure the dots.

No calibration photos yet.

  • The shim's length and width set the scale. Measure a batch with calipers and enter the true size; a 0.01" error here is a 0.2% area error.
  • Mount the phone parallel to the shim on the 1× lens, about 8 to 9 in above it, with the shim's long side along the phone's long side. Turn off flash, portrait mode and macro auto-switch.
  • Use a matte background that is not yellow, tan or wood (mid-gray or blue works). Light it with diffuse light so the brass does not glare.
  • Keep the whole shim in frame and flat. Nothing may cover its outer edge.
